# W3 R44 — prospective response-dependent point loss versus complete-participant selection

**Research-only prospective observation-process diagnostic.** No response-dependent source-selection method is promoted into public inference, and no numerical optimizer pass automatically establishes correct covariance uncertainty, a nominal test or release readiness. This protocol freezes all source-generating seeds, selection probabilities and outcome definitions before the original R44 evidence is inspected.

## Scientific question and targets

[R43](w3-r43-nested-dropout-misspec-results.md) found the stricter physical-coordinate Gaussian REML nuisance optimizer numerically stable under **response-independent anchored point removal**. That experiment did **not** model gaze loss conditional on an unobserved outcome or remove entire participants based on their latent responses. Issue [#296](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/296) remains unresolved.

R44 separates (i) **full-cohort population regression slope** at a fixed prespecified focal time, defined by the original Gaussian generating conditional mean; (ii) **full-cohort realized empirical slope** from the original complete observed time series; (iii) **retained-data empirical slope** from the records that remain visible under each selection mechanism. The last two are descriptive finite-source estimators of potentially different realized populations, **not estimand-equivalent merely because they share an R formula**. Loss and selection mechanisms will not be silently conflated. The full source cohort and independent primitive-block denominator must always be retained in the outcome ledger, even if participants are excluded before modeling.

## Frozen source and observation mechanisms

New independent Gaussian primitive master seed **202610164444** and selection-uniform seed **202610164445**. Use **four independent Gaussian innovation blocks**, nested participant prefixes n=24/48, paired ordinary/extreme predictor support and genuine zero/positive source random-slope variation. Generating exact-zero states remove the actual slope innovation before response reconstruction. These are **32 block/size/support/state source variants**, not 32 independent primitive source units.

Every source variant is paired across four observation processes applied to the same complete latent Gaussian trajectories:

1. **Complete:** every originally recorded native timestamp, trial and participant remains in the regression.
2. **Predictor/time-conditioned point loss (MAR-like):** candidate native interior point retention follows `p=0.76 - 0.12|x|/(1+|x|) - 0.12t`; predictor and native time are assumed observed even when a gaze measurement is absent. Fixed first/last trial endpoints are retained. This is missingness conditionally ignorable with respect to the missing gaze outcome under the synthetic design, but its anchored rescue constraints distinguish it from unconditional MCAR and do not prove ignorability of any future analysis.
3. **Outcome-dependent point loss (MNAR):** candidate interior point retention follows `p=0.70 - 0.25 tanh(y)` for the original *potentially missing* first-dimensional gaze outcome `y`. Unobserved values at dropped times directly determine their observation indicator, and are supplied **only to the missingness generator**, never the fitted estimator. Native endpoints are always retained and each retained trial has at least four native observations.
4. **Latent-outcome-dependent whole-participant selection:** for every source participant, compute the original complete latent mean of their first-dimensional gaze across all native trials and use one participant-level Bernoulli draw with `p=0.60 - 0.30 tanh(mean full latent y)`. Include **all trials and time points** if the participant is selected; exclude **every trial/time point** otherwise. No redraw or participant substitution is allowed when a small sample is retained; explicit minimum-of-six-participants fit refusals are preserved. This is selection on the unobserved complete response, not pointwise dropout.

For the point-loss processes, the time anchors always remain and an explicit deterministic minimum-of-four-point rescue applies. The missingness-generating random uniforms are keyed by original participant and trial IDs so that paired zero/positive source states and nested participant prefixes are reproducible, although their **outcome-dependent probabilities and resulting retained records can legitimately differ**. No interpolation is permitted; the retained data contain only exact original native measurements. Complete-unit selection is not treated as a special case of point-loss weighting. Report original participant counts, retained participants, native trials, native timestamps and response-dependence flags by source.

These are synthetic, explicitly controlled mechanisms; they do not establish that any observed real eye-tracking dropout is MAR or MNAR, nor that the full latent gaze values can be reconstructed from the retained records.

## Numerical and descriptive outcomes

On each observed source, calculate an **observed-only Legendre-five weighted least-squares focal slope contrast** at `TIME[FOCAL]`, alongside the original **full-cohort realized slope** and the generating **true focal conditional mean slope**. Report retained-minus-full-cohort realized-slope and retained-minus-generating-truth differences descriptively by missingness process. There are **no inferential p-values, confidence intervals, bootstrapped significance tests, calibration power claims or corrected selection models in R44**. Do not interpret paired simulations with only four independent primitive source blocks as independent evidence of population-level bias magnitude.

Separately refit the same original **predictor-scaled Gaussian restricted likelihood nuisance kernels** on the **retained observed records only**, under fixed participant random-slope variance ratios exactly 0 and 0.75 (relative to the observed-only reference). Use the same single frozen difficult near-lower intercept nuisance start, original R40 raw direct-positive-linear nuisance L-BFGS-B and strict R41 physical-gradient L-BFGS-B restart. Retain each solver return, Gaussian NLL and projected KKT score. The original **0.001** tolerance for within-pair observed NLL agreement and physical projected KKT is unchanged.

**Full planned denominator:** 4 independent primitive blocks ×2 nested n×2 support×2 generating slope states = **32 primitive-block source variants**, ×4 observation mechanisms = **128 paired observation-process source cases**, ×2 fixed random slope ratios = **256 numerical profiles**, ×2 numerical stages = **512 optimizer-stage records**. Source failures, missingness refusals, missing-source rows, downstream not-attempted stages, KKT exceedances and inferior likelihoods are all negative outcomes and remain in the original full ledger.

An R44 **numerical-only stage gate** can pass only with all source variants, observation processes, profiles and planned solver slots recorded, no source failures, and a given method converged under every mechanism with best observed within-pair NLL difference ≤0.001 and physical projected KKT score ≤0.001. A numerical gate pass says **nothing** about the retained data identifying the original full-population scientific estimand or coverage of CR2 coefficient confidence intervals.

## Scientific boundary and next stage

Distinguish any numeric solver success from **unbiasedness, valid selection adjustment and nominal sampling coverage**. A likelihood optimizer may be first-order stationary while fitted regression targets or residual covariance are biased under nonignorable missingness. R44 examines missingness and selection mechanism consequences for the observed dataset, **not** how an analyst can estimate unobserved selection probabilities from data, and **not** whether complete-participant selection is ignorable. A future controlled R45 can predeclare a selection-adjustment policy and independently replicate pointwise coefficient CI coverage against a fixed full-cohort target, with an explicit separate function-wide simultaneous 90% coverage study.
