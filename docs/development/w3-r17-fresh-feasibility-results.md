## Scientific interpretation and strict limits

The mean midpoint-contrast Satterthwaite reference df is markedly reduced under extreme predictor leverage, especially when outcome covariance is predictor-dependent. This is consistent with concentrated participant information and motivates larger confirmatory source simulation. It must **not** be interpreted as evidence that CR2+Satterthwaite restores nominal 90% coverage: the n=8 independent generating blocks yield extremely wide Monte Carlo intervals. Comparators using the known generating covariance are simulation-only. Correct working targets are provided from the generator and **not estimated from empirical measurements**. Changing to diagonal misspecification does not isolate all possible covariance errors. Comparisons are *pointwise* at one original native time and do not qualify simultaneous functional bands.

The original source passed the independent R16 WLS coefficient and known-source Gaussian variance checks, while the blockwise estimator passed the dense-reference preflight. Exact-head general full CI after documentation additions must be checked separately. An artifact ledger, source SHA, summary and SHA256SUMS were generated by the original scientific job; independent artifact download/checksum verification remains separate.
