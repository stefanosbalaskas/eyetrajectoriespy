## B10 conditional single-change location posterior

![Known split posterior from a declared scalar functional projection](../assets/research/b10-projected-one-break-posterior.svg)

The model projects complete whole-trial functional curves onto a **prespecified fixed direction**, then analytically integrates independent Gaussian segment means under one-break and no-break alternatives with known residual SD. The graph shows posterior split-location probabilities **conditional on exactly one break**, alongside the injected truth. This is not Li–Ghosal's general functional changepoint procedure, does not allow serial correlation/multiple breaks, and carries no calibration guarantee.


## B6 learned population mean and covariance from native Gibbs

![Known synthetic functional population truth versus the learned Gibbs posterior mean and covariance diagonal](../assets/research/b6-learned-population-mean-covariance.svg)

Unlike the fixed-population B6 conditional score benchmark, this figure is generated by the actual **finite-dictionary Gaussian functional-factor Gibbs sampler** on native irregular observations. It learns both the population mean and a low-rank population covariance kernel at fixed component count, prior scales and observation-noise SD. Intervals show uncalibrated **pointwise posterior content**, not a statistically qualified 90% repeated-sampling guarantee. The factor loading curves remain nonidentifiable up to rotations/signs and cannot be interpreted as ordered eigenfunctions without further work.
