# W3 R55 — prospectively frozen Gaussian REML active-face transition profiles

**Research-only discrete deterministic perturbation-path mechanism study. It is not a new independent Gaussian sample, a zero-variance boundary study, a nominal 90% confidence interval method, or a release gate.**

## Scientific question and source provenance

R53 (draft PR #357) found **8/8** original physical Gaussian REML fits numerically accepted, but **4/8** smooth-interior REML nuisance Jacobians refused at the positive participant-slope lower variance-ratio bound. R54 (draft PR #358), independently audited in #359, found **8/8** original selected positive active/free-face local derivatives numerically eligible; all **16** small perturbation directions and **32** full Gaussian REML plus/minus refits stayed on their original active faces and matched the local derivative closely.

That local result does **not** show that the selected Gaussian covariance active face remains stable for responses farther from the originally observed values. **R55 tests how the selected physical Gaussian covariance constraint face changes on a prespecified grid of larger weighted-response perturbations**, and whether the smooth-on-current-face linearized studentized focal CR2 statistic becomes less predictive across levels. It does not infer statistical switching probabilities from independent outcomes.

**Deliberately reuse** the original R53 Gaussian source master seed **202610175353**, all four original Gaussian primitive innovation blocks with n24 participants and native irregular repeated gaze trials, including paired ordinary- and extreme-dependent predictor leverage. There are **eight original source/support cases but ZERO newly generated independent Gaussian source blocks**. Do not describe the paired variants or perturbation points as independent Monte Carlo trials. The source-generating positive participant-slope covariance and native-design projected true focal coefficient remain fixed for evaluating each observed CR2 pivot; the actual Gaussian oracle covariance must never enter a fitted estimator.

## Frozen response-path design

New independent standard-normal weighted-response **direction seed 202610175555**. For each of the eight source/support cases generate **two** fixed, unit-RMS-length native response directions, with source-specific seed offset `100003×block +711×support_index+direction_index`. Normalize each vector to weighted native-row Euclidean length `sqrt(number_of_native_rows)`. Hold that direction fixed throughout its entire *signed* perturbation path.

Compute the observed original weighted-response RMS `rms=sqrt(mean((sqrt(w)y)^2))`. For each direction perturb native weighted response as `y_w ± h d`, using exactly the **four frozen relative step magnitudes**

`h/max(rms,0.01) ∈ {0.0005, 0.005, 0.05, 0.25}`.

The four levels progress by predeclared order and are **not** altered if the first transition is not observed. Do not adaptively halve, add levels, resimulate or condition the grid on any numerical outcome. Both signs are mandatory at every level, yielding **8 original source cases ×2 directions ×4 levels ×2 signs=128 prespecified perturbed Gaussian REML fit slots**, plus **8 original fits = 136 planned accepted/refused fit outcomes and 272 planned individual optimizer start outcomes**.

Every refit calls the *unchanged* R49 two-initialization positive physical covariance Gaussian REML model with the original outcome-dependent WLS residual-variance reference, same ratio limits `LOW=exp(-13.8)`, `HIGH=exp(7)`, original strict optimizer flag/objective-reconstruction rule, physically projected KKT `≤0.001`, and NLL within `≤0.001` of the observed two-start best. Record both numerical starts and their original KKT/NLL facts even when the other start is preferred. Refuse absent or nonfinite covariance/CR2 output; do not change model, regularize Hessian, assume global REML optimality, discard a failure or redraw it.

An *active-face signature* is the ordered sets of participant-intercept, predictor-scaled participant-slope, trial and sensor variance ratios within the **original R54 relative tolerance 1e−5** of their lower/upper ratio bounds. Recompute the signature from each perturbed **selected** fit's physical components and its **own observed WLS reference variance**, not from a fixed original absolute variance bound. For every accepted perturbed fit, record whether its selected active face differs from the original source fit's signature. Separately record any selected optimizer initialization change (`interior` or `spread`): **a start label changing is not proof of a different local optimum**.

## Frozen transition and local-linear diagnostic rules

For each original source, direction and sign, report the **first observed active-face change on the fixed four-level grid**. If a changed face is first seen at level `h_k` and **all earlier levels on that same sign path successfully retained the original face**, report a **discrete observation bracket** `(previous_unchanged_level, first_changed_level]`, starting from the original source at level 0. Do not call the bracket an exact switch threshold or assume there were no off-grid face changes or changes/reversions between sampled levels. If any fit/CR2 refusal occurs before a detected transition, mark the path's first transition **unresolved after refusal**; never skip the failure and form a misleading bracket from surviving levels. If no changed active face is observed at any of the four levels, record **no change on frozen grid only**, not proof of global stability.

Where R54's original **strictly complementary positive active/free-face** derivative is numerically admissible, precompute the unchanged local response Jacobian of the Gaussian REML covariance and studentized CR2 pivot. For each step/direction/sign calculate the linearly extrapolated **physical variance-component vector** and the **true-source-focal-centered studentized CR2 pivot**, using the observed local Jacobian at the original source. Refit all four Gaussian nuisance components on the actual perturbed observed outcomes and recompute the original R22 CR2 point and standard error. Report the **absolute difference of predicted versus observed pivot**, and the **relative Euclidean difference of predicted versus observed physical nuisance components normalized to original/perturbed component size**. These are descriptive numerical errors, not a population-calibrated confidence interval. When the source's local derivative refuses, retain the original fit and the entire path; report local predictions as unavailable, not zero.

**Compare predicted errors descriptively by frozen step level, leverage stratum, and whether the selected face changed.** A same-face observation at large step does not imply the local Taylor approximation is accurate. A changed-face observation establishes that the original face's local derivative is generally inappropriate across that selected active-set transition, but the grid does not isolate whether a jump came from KKT complementarity, numerical root choice, global objective branch switching, or other nuisance nonlinearities. Do not assert one mechanism without separate evidence.

## Explicit inference and release boundaries

R55 can establish **where the selected constrained nuisance fit does or does not remain on the original active face under selected deterministic weighted-response stress directions**, and whether original constrained Taylor predictions deteriorate with perturbation magnitude. It cannot estimate the probability of switching under the genuine latent Gaussian sampling process or determine the true zero-variance null distribution. This study leaves unresolved the full joint estimated-covariance/numerator/CR2-quadratic sampling law, 90% pointwise and simultaneous whole-function intervals, variance-component-null Type-I error/power, covariance-model misspecification and response-driven MAR/MNAR or whole-participant loss.
