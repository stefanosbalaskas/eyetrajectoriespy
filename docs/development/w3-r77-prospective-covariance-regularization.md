# W3 R77 — prospective data-estimated covariance regularization falsification

**Research only. Frozen before any R77 generating-source outcomes.** R76/R76-A (drafts #402/#403) established a finite-sample information-concentration problem in synthetic Gaussian W3 inference: at two extreme high-noise independent units, the slope-weighted HC3 Gaussian simultaneous-band reference included the known 31×2 slope in only **1/32** independently generated curve samples and **11/32** repeated-participant samples, despite a near-one *average* HC3/true marginal-SE ratio. At 16 extreme independent units, inclusion rose to **26/32** curves and **24/32** participants. Both source and independent numerical audits passed, and no method was qualified as nominal 90%.

## Question and estimands

Can **source-observable, fixed-strength positive-definite regularization of the HC3 functional covariance** reduce sensitivity to individual noisy grid cells when independent influential-unit support is scarce, without supplying simulation-known covariance to the estimator? This is a prespecified *candidate-method falsification*, not a procedure to tune on observed coverage. Changing covariance regularization also changes simultaneous reference criticals and marginal scale; each must be reported separately.

For independent unit i, with slope OLS design weight a_i, hat h_i and residual r_i(t,d), define the **unchanged** R74/R76 slope HC3 rectangular factor
`F_ik = a_i r_ik /(1-h_i)`, k=1,…,62, and `C_HC3 = F^T F`.
Let `v_k=C_HC3,kk` and `v_smooth(t,d)` be a **fixed 7-time-point edge-truncated equally weighted** moving mean of `v` *within each dimension*, never across dimensions. For **two frozen shrinkage strengths**, `lambda ∈ {0.25, 0.50}`, construct:

`C_lambda = (1-lambda)*C_HC3 + lambda*diag(v_smooth)`.

The diagonal element of `C_lambda` is `(1-lambda)*v_k+lambda*v_smooth,k`. Inference candidate draws `Z∼N(0,C_lambda)`, **standardized by sqrt(diag(C_lambda))**, and takes the *higher* empirical 90th percentile of `max_k |Z_k/sqrt(C_lambda,kk)|`. Each candidate is data-estimated, positive-definite when all smoothed diagonal entries are positive, and easy to implement numerically; **its statistical validity is not assured**. This does not claim a general high-dimensional covariance shrinkage optimum. The fixed 7-time smoother is particularly restrictive for smooth, nonstationary, or asynchronous gaze; its strength/width will **not** be chosen after viewing results.
