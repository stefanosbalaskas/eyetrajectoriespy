# R35 — exact-zero random-slope Gaussian REML likelihood profiles

**Scientific numerical identifiability diagnostic, research only—not a classical likelihood-ratio hypothesis test, pointwise coverage, or a release gate.** The immediately preceding [R34 original nuisance information study](w3-r34-nuisance-information-results.md) found that 10/16 observed REML nuisance-curvature inverse diagnostics must be **refused** at fitted component boundaries despite zero CR2 derivative failures and convergence of the observed Gaussian REML estimator. Nine refusals involved near-zero participant slope variance. Standard smooth interior Hessian approximations are especially untrustworthy at a variance-component zero boundary.

## Prespecified new source experiment

New master seed **202610163535**, **four independently generated Gaussian source blocks**, n24 participants, predictor-dependent Gaussian random-slope generating covariance, ordinary versus extreme (x0=3,x1=−2.5) predictor support, original native irregular trials/timestamps and one predeclared **correctly matched predictor-scaled** working covariance family. Exactly **eight original source/model fits**, each with **seven predeclared random-slope variance profile points**: slope variance `theta_slope / observed_reference_variance` fixed to **0, 1e−6, 1e−4, 1e−2, 0.1, 1, 10**. Total **56 prespecified constrained optimization tasks**, but only FOUR genuinely independent source blocks; repeated profile points and ordinary/extreme designs are paired, not independent observations. Grid span is exploratory and finite—it must NOT be treated as covering a global likelihood maximum or a calibrated interval.

Every source first receives the existing independent Gaussian R25 observed-only REML fit (interior-constrained `log(theta)` parameterization). The new **independent profile objective** then fixes the random-slope component at each predeclared nonnegative absolute variance level and **reoptimizes the other three covariance components** (participant intercept, repeated-trial, sensor) by restricted Gaussian likelihood with analytic gradient in their log ratios, bounded as in R25. Critically, **`theta_slope=0` is represented as mathematical zero**, not a tiny positive lower bound, numerical variance floor, or a pseudo-nonzero estimate. Other components remain strictly positive so original participant covariance matrices can be checked using Cholesky. Report optimizer nonconvergence or numerical refusal separately for every fixed profile point.

## Independent objective/gradient numerical contracts

When the fixed random-slope variance is positive, compare this new restricted objective and the free-three analytic score against the **original separately independently validated R25** restricted negative Gaussian log-likelihood and score at the *same* four-component variance vector. At exactly zero, verify that the restricted likelihood is numerically finite and that the analytic free-three score agrees with independent central finite differences of the objective. The objective must approach its exactly-zero value smoothly as the fixed variance approaches zero from above; however, no standard chi-square reference for testing a boundary component is allowed.

For each original source case, report the fixed-grid minimum, original unconstrained-but-bounded REML nuisance estimate/likelihood, whether exactly zero is the lowest observed **grid** likelihood, the 2×negative log-likelihood gaps *relative to the best of seven evaluated grid points only*, and every other nuisance component's fitted value and boundary status. A selected grid minimum is not a global restricted-likelihood optimum. The estimator only sees originally observed trajectories and does not receive simulator covariance truth or latent generating random effects.

## Statistical interpretation and no-release rule

Likelihood profiles can expose flat regions, nonidentifiability or boundary solutions obscured by unconstrained Gaussian log-variance optimization. They cannot by themselves establish calibrated variance-component confidence intervals, especially at a nonregular zero boundary. No naive `chi-square(1)` threshold, chi-bar-square mixture, one-sided nuisance CI or method winner is authorized from this exploratory eight-case numeric grid. Such boundary-distribution references require explicit assumptions and independent validation, particularly when other variance components may also be weakly identified.

No functional regression effect CI, estimated-working-covariance CR2/Satterthwaite df correction, 90% pointwise truth-inclusion study, or simultaneous bands are built. Keep [#289](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/289) and [#296](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/296) OPEN; historical original R20 41/256 failures, R22 32 CR2 refusals, R33 four inclusion disagreements and R34 10 boundary refusals unchanged. All PRs draft, protected main/API, release tags and PyPI untouched.
