## Exact independent Gaussian generating law and source design

- **NEW** independent primitive source seed **202610197171**; **32 primitive blocks** each with two independent-unit designs (72 independent complete curves; or 36 independent participants × three complete repeated trials) and two paired predictor leverage supports (ordinary condition independently drawn uniform(−0.95,0.95), extreme *first and last unit conditions overwritten* with −4 and +4). Predictor supports reuse the same Gaussian response innovations and independent-unit conditions within block/design, while the mean response is recomputed for the actual predictor. This gives **128 source fits**, 32 independent primitive source block indices per design/support, **not 128 independent coverage trials per cell**.
- The fixed functional truth is `truth_coefficients(TIME)` from the original W3 protocol: 31 time grid entries, two dimensions, intercept and slope. Conditional on design `X=[1,x]` and known Gaussian source law, the OLS slope coefficient vector satisfies `beta_hat_slope−beta_true_slope | X ~ N(0, ((X'X)^−1)[1,1] × C_unit)`, exactly (not asymptotically). Ordinary curves have **independent N(0,0.04²) errors per time×dimension and curve**. Participant unit means have **independent participant random intercept deviations N(0,0.065²) per dimension multiplied by (1+0.35 t)**, plus independent trial noise **N(0,0.04²)/3** for the mean of three trials. Distinct dimensions are independent. No response dropout or source heteroskedasticity.
- Consequently, the **true conditional slope SE** at every time×dimension is exactly `sqrt(((X'X)^−1)[1,1])*sqrt(C_unit[t,d;t,d])`. The true Gaussian standardized slope **maximum absolute pivot** is independent of `X`; only the correlation of participant errors across the time grid matters. This structure can support a *known-law benchmark* conditional on predictor/leverage, not an estimator applicable to real observed gaze data.
- Oracle reference seed **202610197172**, **32,767** independent Gaussian standardized max-statistic draws *per design*, **65,534 total reference draws**; generate once per design and preserve their maximum-statistic arrays in the final archive. Use the frozen **higher empirical 90% quantile** of these draws as the known-law Gaussian reference critical. The source-law oracle is approximate only through its explicitly recorded Monte Carlo quantile; the underlying Gaussian covariance and studentization are exact.

## Scientific interpretation and release boundary

If the Gaussian **known-law** oracle bands show near-nominal 90% inclusion while original wild HC1 continues to undercover, it would localize a **studentization and/or resampling-calibration problem under a correctly specified Gaussian source**, but does not identify a uniquely valid data-estimated replacement. Conversely, if original oracle truth inclusion is unexpectedly low, re-evaluate source-generation/conditional covariance assumptions and Monte Carlo uncertainty before interpreting it.

**The known covariance is unavailable in real data**, where gaze loss, non-Gaussian tails, heteroskedasticity, irregular repeated trials and model misspecification need further tests. Do not claim the known-Gaussian-law band is a production method. R71 must subsequently undergo a **separate independent original-data numerical audit** of the source-level truth pivots, all original 999 wild maxima and the archived oracle reference maxima. Engineering-green simulation cannot alone resolve nominal 90% undercoverage [#289] or response-dependent gaze observation [#296].
