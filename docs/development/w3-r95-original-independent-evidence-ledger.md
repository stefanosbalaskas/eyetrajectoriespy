## Critical scientific restrictions

The latent response direction is *not* identified by the known randomized invitation probability or observed returned outcomes. A point-identified parameter under assumed $c_1=c_0$ is **not** evidence that equality holds in nature. Equal observational likelihood across distinct latent laws is verified, so no method based solely on this observable event distribution can consistently distinguish those latent laws in general.

All original/audit research source remains draft/unmerged, the public package/API and stable 1.1.0 are untouched, and v1.2/GitHub/PyPI release is on hold.
