## Frozen representative results

For matched original-source **50% invitations**, the nominal uniform-completion adjusted HT oracle conditional design biases were:

| Source law | 1% selective failure | 5% selective failure | 15% selective failure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Independent curves · MAR | −0.000250 | −0.001300 | −0.004359 |
| Independent curves · MNAR | −0.000325 | −0.001695 | −0.005683 |
| Repeated-trial participants · MAR | −0.000258 | −0.001343 | −0.004502 |
| Repeated-trial participants · MNAR | −0.000369 | −0.001924 | −0.006452 |

The **true uniform-completion** source expectation for the nominally adjusted estimator has exact zero oracle conditional bias for all these nominal failure levels. Under outcome-selective failure the latent full-source oracle bias is nonzero, but the realized eight-draw empirical error need not agree in sign or magnitude due to randomization. These source-level values do **not** estimate a hidden completion propensity from observed data. A small observed RMSE does not validate uniform response.

For example, at 50% audit assignment and 5% failure, the original nominal-corrected finite-source empirical RMSE was approximately **0.01538** under selective versus **0.01543** under uniform completion for MAR independent curves, and approximately **0.01249** versus **0.01211** for MNAR repeated-trial participants. Similar RMSE cannot establish return-law equivalence.
