# R37 — continuously optimized Gaussian REML variance profile at exact zero

**Research-only analytic and optimizer reference, not a calibrated likelihood-ratio significance test or functional coefficient confidence interval.** [R36 original source](w3-r36-split-null-profile-results.md) used an explicitly finite seven-point grid for a true-zero versus positive-variance source calibration pilot. Its held-out decisions were 0/8 null and 0/8 positive under ordinary support, 2/8 null and 2/8 positive under extreme support; threshold calibration had only eight independent null blocks per support. These negative results do not establish either test calibration or useful discrimination, and the discrete grid is NOT a globally optimized nonnegative-variance likelihood ratio.

## Source and predeclared mathematical task

R37 independently codes a Gaussian restricted negative loglikelihood objective and **four analytic gradients** in a mixed parameterization: three strictly positive nuisance log-variance ratios (participant intercept, trial variance, sensor variance) and **one LINEAR nonnegative participant-slope variance ratio that allows mathematical zero without an artificial positive floor**. The exact slope score at zero is obtained directly from Gaussian blockwise REML traces and projected residual quadratic forms. At strictly positive slope variance, both the objective and gradient are independently compared to the previously validated R25 log-variance REML reference; at exactly zero, the direct slope score is compared against a one-sided independent numerical derivative. R35's exact-zero nuisance-profile evaluator remains a separate numerical reference.

New master seed **202610163737**, **four independently generated original source blocks**, n24, ordinary versus extreme predictors (x0=3,x1=−2.5) under predictor-dependent source covariance and true-zero/positive variance source states. The true-zero source rebuilds original native observed gaze trajectories without the participant random-slope Gaussian innovation; positive source uses the exact same participant intercept, trial and sensor primitive Gaussian draws, trial IDs, timepoints and predictors, adding back that participant slope. **16 original source/state models but only four independent primitive Gaussian source blocks**, not 16 independent experiments.

Each observed-only source receives the independent R25 Gaussian REML initializer; four predeclared continuously optimized Gaussian negative restricted-likelihood starts are run using original L-BFGS-B gradients: slope ratios **0, .01, 1 and 10**. Free log-variance ratios retain original R25 numerical bounds, slope ratio constrained in **[0,100]**; this high upper limit and any active upper bound are disclosed, not treated as unconstrained infinity. Record all optimizer nonconvergence, objective values, boundary solutions and between-start discrepancies. In parallel, fit the exact original **seven frozen R35 slope profile grid points** with the other nuisance components reoptimized. The selected best objective is only the lowest of **four successfully optimized starts**, not an independent proof of global optimality.

Require the best successful continuous optimizer objective to be no larger (to numerical tolerance) than the separately evaluated fixed-grid minimum. This is a **numerical consistency reference**, not a scientific significance test. Preserve mismatches as failures/diagnostics rather than quietly accepting a continuum MLE label.

## Scientific interpretation and prohibited shortcuts

At exact zero, there is no log slope variance, hence the continuous linear slope-ratio gradient is essential: exponentiating a lower-bounded log variance is **not equivalent** to modeling zero. A stable original likelihood/gradient implementation is prerequisite to future boundary-aware likelihood-ratio or parametric-bootstrap qualification; it cannot itself show nominal type-I error or coefficient coverage. A multistart bounded optimizer can converge reproducibly while still lacking a global optimality guarantee, especially under weakly identified covariance components or high leverage.

No standard chi-square/mixture reference, p-values, variance-component confidence intervals, nuisance-estimation-corrected functional CR2/Satterthwaite inference, 90% pointwise coefficient truth coverage, or whole-function simultaneous confidence bands are authorized. A much larger independent true-zero/calibration-and-heldout programme would be needed before considering even a continuous profile statistic as a validated test. Preserve original R20 41/256 failures, R22 32 numerical refusals, R33 four inclusion disagreements, R34 10 boundary-Hessian refusals and R35–R36 zero-profile results unchanged.
