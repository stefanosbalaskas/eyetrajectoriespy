# W3 R73 — prospective predictor-dependent heteroskedastic Gaussian band stress test

**Research-only design frozen before original R73 source outcomes.** Scientific blocker [#289](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/289) stays OPEN; no v1.2 release or public inference API.

## Why this particular experiment

The 32-new-block [R72 original #392](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/392) and independently original-data-audited [R72-A #393](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/393) found severe wild-HC1 undercoverage under high leverage, with much improved observed 90% full-grid slope inclusion under a **data-estimated common HC2 covariance Gaussian** reference. Extreme leverage: curves **3/32 wild, 26/32 HC2-estimated, 28/32 known-law**; participants **10/32 wild, 29/32 HC2, 29/32 known-law**. R72 is a *common unit covariance* Gaussian simulation, so an HC2 pooled covariance may be inappropriate when each unit's noise variance depends on the predictor. R73 tests that assumption rather than changing method thresholds after seeing source outcomes.

## New Gaussian generating law, independence and workload

- Freeze **32 NEW independent primitive Gaussian generating blocks**, source seed **202610197373**, disjoint from R71/R72. Each primitive block generates two independent-unit designs: **72 independent curves** and **36 independent participants × 3 complete trials**. Each design has **ordinary** predictor support x ~ Uniform(−0.95, 0.95) and **extreme** support with the first and final independent-unit predictors overwritten with −4, +4. The two supports reuse the underlying primitive participant and trial Gaussian innovations.
- **Two prespecified variance regimes, paired on those same sources:** `constant` unit scale s_i=1; `predictor_heteroskedastic` scale s_i=1+0.75 |x_i|. Both participant random effects and independent trial noise are multiplied by s_i **for the corresponding source unit**. Under extreme support s_i=4 for the two ±4 predictor units. The functional mean is recomputed under each x but the underlying unscaled Gaussian innovations are unchanged. Fixed W3 functional truth at the original observed 31×2 grid and original base laws: curve noise SD 0.04; participant random intercept SD 0.065 ×(1+0.35t) and trial noise SD 0.04, mean across three trials. Independent units remain Gaussian and independent; cross-time participant correlation remains, but their covariance matrices are *not identical* in the heteroskedastic regime.
- **256 paired source datasets = 32 generating blocks × 2 designs × 2 supports × 2 variance regimes.** These are **32** genuinely independent generating block indices per design/support/regime, NOT 256 independent draws for a single method. Four eight-block source shards, failure-inclusive source archive and immutable per-shard SHA256, fixed original scientific checkout.
- Freeze estimator and numerical rules from R72: unchanged public W3 OLS/HC1 + **999 unit-level Rademacher wild** draws per fitted dataset; separately recompute original W3 coefficient/HC1 numerical oracles within 2e−10. Compare original R72 *unmodified* pooled independent-unit HC2 common-covariance Gaussian factor/bootstrap method, **999 conditionally Gaussian draws per original source**. Freeze wild seed **202610197374**, estimated-Gaussian seed **202610197375**, both disjoint from R72. Each method uses the original `higher` empirical 90th percentile; **511,488 conditional maximum-statistic draws total** (256×999×2). These draws are NOT independently generated sources.
- A separate **analytic simulation-known heterogeneous Gaussian slope oracle** is permitted *only as benchmark*: conditional on each X, independent-unit errors have covariance s_i² C_base, giving slope covariance `sum_i [((X'X)^−1 X'_i)_slope]² s_i² C_base`. Its true SE has this exact scalar factor times the true base covariance diagonal; because the s_i are *scalar for every time×dimension on unit i*, the standardized functional correlation is exactly the base design-specific Gaussian time correlation, independent of the leverage and heteroskedastic scales. Retain the R71 **32,767 original standardized Gaussian max-statistic law draws per design** and its 90% critical for a deterministic benchmark. **Do not pass this true covariance to HC2 estimator or wild reference.**
- Archive every source's full independent-unit `X`, original `Y`, truth slope, unit scale vector, HC1, HC2 and true-oracle slope full-grid standard-error arrays, 999 original wild maximum-statistic and 999 estimated-Gaussian maximum-statistic draws, all three source truth-inclusion classifications, all three criticals and observed pivots, fit errors/refusals, hat leverage and source provenance. No omitted source or hidden correction.

## Prespecified primary outcomes and fail-closed statistical gates

Primary qualitative falsification: among **predictor_heteroskedastic** sources with **extreme support**, compare complete 90% grid slope truth inclusion under estimated HC2 Gaussian, original wild HC1 and the *known heterogeneous covariance* benchmark, independently for curve and participant designs. Use **32 source blocks as the denominator** per method/condition, report Wilson 95% uncertainty, source-paired candidate-minus-original and known-minus-candidate discordances, full-denominator refusal bounds. Compare the HC2 estimated slope SE to the *heteroskedastic true* slope SE (not the common covariance R71 formula), and report whole-grid critical-value ratios. The constant regime is an internal concurrent baseline, not 32 new independent calibration confirmations across methods.

**No method selection from the four configurations**, no reweighting, threshold tuning, source refusal deletion or new seed after seeing results. ## Interpretive boundaries

Under heteroskedasticity, the R72 residual-pooled `C_hat_HC2=(1/n)Σ r_i r_i'/(1−h_i)` estimates a mixture of independent-unit covariances, **not the correct slope-weighted heteroskedastic covariance**. R73 can falsify whether the empirically promising R72 bands retain useful coverage when that assumption is violated; it cannot establish broad heteroskedastic robustness, 90% calibrated inference, or any response-dependent gaze loss. Source covariance is proportional across time for each unit here; more complex source covariance heterogeneity, unequal numbers of trials, non-Gaussian tails, smaller n and MAR/MNAR are outside R73 and need later separate prospective studies.
