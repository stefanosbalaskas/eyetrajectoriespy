!!! danger "Conditional scalar model learning, NOT field MNAR identification or 90% inference"
    R84 supplies **truly external synthetic source observations** for randomly selected originally missing values. That is compatible with noisy recovery, source-level dependence and the visible-only pilot being an imperfect conditional-mean specification; the experiment has no interval or consistency guarantee.

## Scientific conclusions that remain unsupported

The archive reproducibly answers whether *a particular parametric selection score*, using a visible-only pilot and additional independent synthetic missing-response ascertainment, yields informative **scalar fitted δ values** in these source conditions. It does **not** show that lost gaze can be externally remeasured, that random ascertainment can be implemented on a real participant's historical gaze, or that the recovered-outcome model remains valid with covariate-dependent validation, misclassified samples, unknown recovery fractions or a misspecified MNAR family.

The conditional scalar coefficient is not the complete time×coordinate functional slope and it supplies no full-function inference covariance. **No nominal 90% calibration, source-independent interval coverage, or nonparametric MNAR identification** is established, even though all original synthetic source diagnostics have been independently recreated.
