# R23 — independent-source estimated-working-covariance identifiability study

**Research-only; no inference coverage evaluation.** R22 ([PR #324](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/324)) established numerical agreement for the scaled-Cholesky CR2 calculation under selected well-conditioned specified targets, including the independent high-precision and R `clubSandwich` comparisons. The historical R20 pilot ([PR #322](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/322)) nevertheless had **41 of 256 original numerical failures**; R22 separately computed nine of those with the new stable factor and deliberately refused 32 extremely ill-conditioned sensor-floor cases. No original failures were reclassified. These numerical tests do not establish the reliability or identifiability of the *estimated working covariance*.

## Frozen fresh research source

Master seed **202610153301**, distinct from R16–R22, and **12 independent generating blocks** (not 576 independently generated studies). Each block supplies nested n=24,48,96 participants, ordinary/extreme participant leverage, predictor-independent/dependent covariance innovation mechanisms, Legendre-5 and cubic-spline-13 fixed mean bases, and two separately fitted *predeclared* working covariance structures (shared random slope and predictor-scaled random slope). **576 paired model-family evaluations** are planned, with *only 12 independently seeded generating blocks*. The same block is reused across design conditions intentionally; no inference rates are calculated or pooled as independent replications.

The nuisance estimator receives **only observable native trial times, trajectories, predictor labels, participant and trial IDs, and WLS residuals**, not the source innovation vectors, generating means, covariance targets or known variance parameters. Parameter recovery is compared with simulation-known component variances *only after estimation*, and only when the prespecified working family has the same structural form as the generator. Estimating both working families is mandatory; no source-oracle-guided family selection. A mis-specified family is characterized by its moment fit and stability, but comparing its component estimates directly against structurally incompatible true parameters is not a valid recovery assessment.

## Required output and statistical distinctions

For all 48 design/family cells (three n × four source conditions × two bases × two families), report **12 original independent generating blocks as denominator**, the number of successful covariance fits, nuisance estimator failures, zero/boundary variance component estimates, sensor-floor intervention frequency, component moment operator rank/conditioning, residual moment-fit diagnostics, and median absolute relative component error for structurally matched families. Each covariance fit feeds the unchanged predeclared R22 scaled-Cholesky numerical factor; report **unconditional numerical CR2 acceptances and explicit refusals** separately. Wilson 95% intervals summarize Monte Carlo uncertainty about *numerical acceptance and sensor-floor frequencies*, **not confidence interval coverage**. Estimator failures and numerically refused fits must remain in all source ledgers.

The exact original source, outcomes and failure identities from R20/R21 remain untouched. This R23 identifiability study does **not** compute nominal pointwise truth inclusion, select a statistical method, calibrate Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, incorporate nuisance covariance estimation uncertainty, establish valid simultaneous bands or qualify external eye-tracking applications. Scientific and publication gates remain closed, with issues #289/#296 still open. No public API, protected-main merge, 1.2 tag or PyPI upload.

## Decision criteria for further statistical inference

Only if the estimator's identifiability, boundary frequency, moment fit and numerically reliable acceptance rates are understood should a **separately preregistered R23/R24 inferential calibration** study investigate repeated-sampling coverage, nuisance-estimation effects, unconditional computation success, and conditional coverage among successful fits, using materially more independent generating blocks and realistic observation-process perturbations. This initial twelve-block experiment is a descriptive feasibility study, not proof of stable component recovery.
