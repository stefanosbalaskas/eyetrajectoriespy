# W3 R51 — prospective mechanism decomposition of narrowed Gaussian nuisance-refit bootstrap-t intervals

**Research-only mechanism experiment, not a new deployable confidence interval, statistical calibration or release gate.** This protocol and every numerical/denominator constraint are fixed before examining new R51 original-source evidence.

## Why R51 is not another broad calibration sweep

R50's complete and independently audited original 24-independent-block Gaussian source showed that high-leverage nuisance-refit bootstrap-t intervals remained materially narrower than correctly specified fitted-REMl conditional CR2 (mean half-width **0.07038 versus 0.09363** with 63 draws), even though both included the generating focal coefficient in **21/24** blocks. The 31-draw bootstrap included in 19/24, recovering two independent-block inclusions with 63 draws. The wrong-covariance-family comparator was narrower again and no method was statistically qualified for a population-level 90% pointwise claim.

The next necessary scientific question is **mechanistic**, not a post hoc choice of the five R50 candidate methods: how much of the short critical-value distribution comes from refitting the Gaussian covariance versus holding its estimates fixed, and how much comes from model generation under the estimated covariance instead of the known simulator covariance? Because the true covariance is unavailable empirically, using it for pseudodata is **an expressly nonimplementable simulation diagnostic** and may not be promoted as an inferential method.

## Frozen independent sources and common-noise paired interventions

New independent Gaussian generating-block master seed **202610165151**, Gaussian pseudodata seed **202610165152**. Generate **eight independent native Gaussian primitive innovation blocks**, each with n=24 participants, native irregular repeated gaze trials, the original Legendre-five fixed mean and **positive predictor-scaled participant Gaussian random slope**. Pair ordinary-dependent and extreme-dependent predictor support *within* each source block. This gives **16 paired source cases but only eight independent primitive blocks per leverage stratum**. The Gaussian innovations are new and distinct from R48–R50; no R50 source outcomes or post hoc previously observed undercoverage block identities are reused. Do not combine paired leverage variants as 16 independent experimental replications.

Estimate all four Gaussian nuisance variance components from the original observed outcome data using the **unchanged R49** two-positive-physical-start Gaussian REML likelihood and original analytic score. Each fit must pass the inherited **max physical projected KKT ≤0.001**, observed-best NLL gap **≤0.001**, objective reconstruction tolerance **≤1e-6**, SPD matrix and full numerical refusal contracts. Preserve both optimizer starts even when one yields an unfavorable NLL. This is a first-order local numerical check, **not global likelihood-optimum proof**.

For each original block/support condition, retain (i) the **original known-covariance oracle conditional Satterthwaite CR2** (simulation-only benchmark) and (ii) the **fitted-covariance conditional Satterthwaite CR2**. Both use the original scaled-Cholesky CR2 WLS contrast and nominal 90% two-sided t critical. The fitted target's df does not account analytically for re-estimating covariance on the same data.

For every integer replicate index **0–62**, freeze **one independent standard-normal vector per native participant block** and use that *same vector* to construct both covariance-generation interventions. The original native observed-data WLS mean and original weighted participant/trial/time/predictor design are held fixed:

**Fitted-covariance generation**, implementable as a fitted parametric model:

- Draw weighted Gaussian pseudo-outcomes with the **observed-data fitted positive Gaussian covariance**. Compute the original scaled-Cholesky CR2 contrast and pointwise standard error using that original fitted covariance held **fixed across replications**, yielding a **fixed-nuisance studentized pivot**.
- On the **identical pseudo-responses**, refit *all four* positive physical Gaussian REML covariance nuisance components under the unchanged two-start physical KKT/NLL gate. Recompute original scaled-Cholesky CR2 and its standard error to obtain the **nuisance-refit studentized pivot**.
- These are a controlled within-pseudodata comparison isolating the consequence of re-estimation in studentization, conditional on the same observed-data fitted Gaussian generating covariance. They do not by themselves prove population coverage.

**Oracle-covariance generation**, **simulation diagnostic only**:

- With precisely the same standard-normal vectors and WLS mean as the fitted-generation intervention, draw Gaussian responses under the **true source-generating covariance**. The true covariance is used **ONLY TO SIMULATE pseudo-outcomes**; it must not be supplied to the nuisance refitting or CR2 working target. Refitting all four nuisance components from the simulated pseudo-outcomes and recomputing original CR2 yields a third family of signed studentized pivots.
- The contrast to the fitted-generation/refit distribution describes sensitivity to the **generation covariance**, not an empirically available confidence interval or a causal attribution to a unique nuisance mechanism. The oracle-generated method must always be labeled **not implementable with real outcomes**.

For all three pivot families, construct the **90th percentile of absolute 63 studentized pivots**, with the frozen linear quantile convention, and use that critical with the original observed fitted CR2 focal point/SE solely for exploratory same-source interval decisions. Maintain exact pseudodata/replicate pairing. If a fixed-pivot or either covariance-refitting branch fails numerically, **all three pseudo-distribution interval arms refuse** for the original source rather than retaining only successful draws or selectively imputing failed pivots. Conditional/oracle t-reference interval arms remain separately reportable even if bootstrap arms refuse.

## Full original evidence and quantitative interpretation

Freeze **8 independently generated primitive blocks ×2 paired leverage = 16 source/support cases ×5 pointwise interval methods = 80 interval records**. There are **63 matched replicate indices ×2 generation covariance conditions ×16 source cases = 2,016 individually recorded Gaussian draw/refit records**. Every fitted-generation draw and oracle-generation draw attempts a separate original four-component Gaussian REML nuisance refit, giving **2,016 expected pseudo-outcome nuisance fits plus 16 original nuisance fits = 2,032**, each with **two prespecified initializations = 4,064 optimizer-start slots**. The fitted-generation/fixed-nuisance CR2 operation uses the *same* pseudo-dataset and does not create an extra nuisance fit. Nonattempted and refused stages remain recorded with the originally expected denominator.

The planned design splits the full original source into fixed **blocks 0–3 and blocks 4–7**. Each shard writes all source/draw/refit/interval slots and a SHA256 of its original case file; its own GitHub Actions PR-merge source checkout is recorded. The aggregator must refuse missing/duplicated shards, unexpected source block/support IDs, inconsistent checkout SHA, unregistered seeds, altered SHA256, or any missing original stage slot. The original evidence includes `cases.json`, `summary.json`, `source-commit.txt` and `SHA256SUMS`; failed numerical refits remain visible, never redrawn or excluded.

For each leverage stratum with *eight independent primitive blocks* and each method, report the original full-denominator generating-truth inclusion numerator (refusals count as noncoverage), mean interval half-width among numerically accepted original cases and Wilson 95% Monte Carlo bounds. More central to R51's mechanism question, report **within-primitive-block paired** median ratios `critical(refitted, fitted-generation) / critical(fixed, fitted-generation)` and `critical(refitted, oracle-generation) / critical(refitted, fitted-generation)`, with directional widths/inclusion disagreements. These paired diagnostics cannot separately identify every source of interval narrowing, because conditional Satterthwaite critical values, fitted nuisance estimates and CR2 geometry interact.

**Eight independently generated primitive blocks, even with 63 pseudodraws per arm, cannot qualify a pointwise 90% confidence interval method or establish general coverage superiority.** This is a new-source method-mechanism feasibility test designed to isolate candidate failure mechanisms and inform whether further mathematical derivation rather than greater brute-force simulation is necessary. Do not initiate a new 256-source statistical-calibration experiment or select a public bootstrap estimator from these paired diagnostics.

A nonparametric bootstrap, an observed-fitted parametric bootstrap and an oracle-generated diagnostic are not interchangeable inferential procedures.
