# External observation validation changes what MNAR models can learn

**Methods companion · 21 October 2026 · W3 R84**

[R83](w3-mnar-selection-sensitivity-r83.md) varied five hypothetical outcome-dependent selection coefficients over the same observed trajectory and correctly concluded that the data alone do not identify which MNAR coefficient generated the missingness. The new [R84 experimental study](w3-external-validation-mnar-identification-r84.md) asks what happens when the analyst receives **actual new information**: independently recovered values for a random subset of outcomes that were originally missing.

!!! warning "External recovery is simulated, not an available feature of the eye tracker"
    R84 does not demonstrate that a gaze sample genuinely lost by an eye tracker can subsequently be reconstructed or measured. The random recovery mechanism is **externally supplied by the complete-data simulator**. It must not be presented as an imputation algorithm, an identified real-data MNAR estimator, or a validated 90% confidence-band method. W3 #289 and #296 remain open, stable 1.1.0 unchanged, version 1.2 unreleased.

## 1. Three fundamentally different operations

An **assumed MNAR coefficient** modifies model weights but does not add responses. An **imputed value** is a guess based on a model rather than a new measurement. An **independently ascertained missing outcome** is actual extra response information that the original observation process did not provide.

R84 simulates only the third operation as a controlled study. The original MAR/MNAR masks remain fixed. A new independent random field externally reveals 0%, 25% or 50% of otherwise missing values. Its positive selection probability is known by design. At zero recovery, R84 returns **no selection-parameter estimate**, not δ=0.

![R84 mean additional missing outcomes recovered under randomized external ascertainment](../assets/research/w3-r84-external-gaze-recovery-counts.svg)

*Figure 1.* These are newly observed gaze coordinate/trial values per original generated dataset, not new independent participants. Nothing analogous is established for an actual eye tracker.

## 2. Learning a restricted scalar parameter requires a measurement and a model

The selection process is frozen as logistic in recorded predictor, time and trial, plus one scalar coefficient δ multiplying a bounded function of the response residual. MAR has true δ=0; MNAR has true δ=1.1. This truth is known only to the synthetic generator.

R84 first fits the response conditional-mean pilot **using originally visible Y only**, separately over time and coordinate. It then uses both original visible outcomes and independently recovered missing values to calculate a weighted selection score. Recovered missing outcomes receive inverse recovery probability weight $1/r$.

The score is solved for **one scalar δ** within frozen bounds [−4,4], with refusal of invalid information or nonconvergence. A distinct independently implemented bisection solver confirms numerical agreement with the original Newton solver. Neither procedure fits a sampling covariance nor provides intervals or whole-trajectory uncertainty.

This can make a **specified parametric selection coefficient estimable conditional on the validity of the external-recovery and pilot/selection models**. It cannot identify the full class of possible outcome-dependent missingness laws. If the apparent recovery were merely generated by an imputation model, it would not provide the same independent information.

## 3. The controls matter

![R84 MAR negative and MNAR positive source control delta estimates under two external recovery fractions](../assets/research/w3-r84-external-recovery-estimated-selection-delta.svg)

*Figure 2.* In the two-anchor heterogeneous-noise cells, newly recovered outcomes let the fitted scalar selection model distinguish its MAR δ=0 negative control from the generating MNAR δ=1.1 positive control. The five assumed coefficients used in R83 are **not** source-identified estimates; R84 is testing a different information setting.

The original R84 source mean MNAR δ estimates with 25/50% recovery are about **1.041/1.025 for curves** and **1.003/.998 for participants**, while MAR source means remain near zero. No fitted estimate exists at 0%.

These results do not demonstrate unbiasedness. The actual generating selection mean is hidden from fitting, whereas R84 uses an estimated visible-only mean; model misspecification and finite independent-unit source variation remain possible.

## 4. More recovered values do not guarantee smaller realized error

![R84 mean absolute MNAR selection coefficient errors across original eight source configurations](../assets/research/w3-r84-validation-fraction-model-error.svg)

*Figure 3.* Finite-source mean absolute δ errors are usually smaller in the 50% recovery arm than in the 25% arm, but **not uniformly**. Under 16 extreme predictor units with predictor-dependent heteroskedasticity, mean absolute error increased slightly in both curves and participants at 50% recovery.

This is not paradoxical evidence that external information is undesirable. Conditional weighting, a deliberately fitted pilot model, finite sample variations, nonlinear bounded scores and a limited n=32 new generating blocks per fixed condition can alter realized errors. Without a validated variance estimator and additional source designs, R84 cannot make statistical precision guarantees.

## 5. Independent experimental units and honest provenance

The original synthetic experiment comprises **32 genuinely new primitive blocks per fixed condition**, 256 complete X/Y source-condition datasets, 512 paired MAR/MNAR masks and **1,536** selection/recovery diagnostic records. The masks and recovery arms reanalyse the **same source**, not 1,536 independently sampled eye-tracking experiments.

[Original R84 science #39581392688](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/actions/runs/39581392688) passed 12/12 dedicated jobs. [R84-A independently coded actual original archive audit #39582618020](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/actions/runs/39582618020) passed 4/4 jobs and verified all source identities, paired masks, nested recovery draws, visible-only pilot fits and weighted selection parameter roots.

The results are numerical evidence concerning **one artificially specified external-validation design**. No real auxiliary measurement source, MNAR identifiability from ordinary incomplete recordings, or statistically calibrated functional confidence band is established.
