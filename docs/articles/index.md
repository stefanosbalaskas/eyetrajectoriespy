# Scientific articles

These short, research-oriented articles connect the software's scientific contracts, examples, figures, and release-governance evidence. The two W3 inference articles describe **unmerged experimental research**; their coverage results are **not product qualification**. They are meant to help select and **interpret** analyses, rather than to promote every available function to a default method.

!!! info "Release boundary"
    **1.1.0** is the published stable distribution. The orchestration layer described here belongs to the completed **1.2 candidate source** while literal `1.2.0rc1` is undergoing separate qualification. Do not assume the ten `run_*_workflow()` calls are installed by `pip install eyetrajectoriespy==1.1.0`.
