# Scientific articles

These short, research-oriented articles connect the software's scientific contracts, examples, figures, and release-governance evidence. They are meant to help select and **interpret** analyses, rather than to promote every available function to a default method.

!!! info "Release boundary"
    **1.1.0** is the published stable distribution. The orchestration layer described here belongs to the completed **1.2 candidate source** while literal `1.2.0rc1` is undergoing separate qualification. Do not assume the ten `run_*_workflow()` calls are installed by `pip install eyetrajectoriespy==1.1.0`.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **1. Choosing among ten functional gaze workflows**

  Select a workflow from the observation grid, scientific outcome, sampling hierarchy, and required uncertainty target. Includes a ten-route comparison table, selection traps and links to method-specific guidance.

  [Read the article](choosing-the-ten-workflows.md)

- **2. Interpreting FPCA, regression, recurrence and audit figures**

  Understand what the generated synthetic figures illustrate, what they cannot establish, and which plots need uncertainty/sensitivity support before publication.

  [Read the article](interpreting-workflow-figures.md)

- **3. Reproducible workflow bundles**

  Follow a typed result through recorded choices, ordered steps, manuscript reporting, machine-readable tables, portable results and SHA-256 verification.

  [Read the article](reproducible-workflow-bundles.md)

- **4. Bayesian FPCA: the B4 feasibility boundary**

  Distinguish the definitive external-comparator results from the claims that have **not** been established about architecture selection, uncertainty calibration or software promotion.

  [Read the article](bayesian-feasibility-boundary.md)

</div>
