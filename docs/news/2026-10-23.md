# 23 October 2026 — W3 evidence update

<small>Research notes · Independent mathematical audits · Not a package release</small>

## What changed

The programme progressed from empirical stress-testing of functional confidence bands to a precise **identifiability** question. In an originally missing binary-outcome stratum, what can be learned about unreturned outcomes using observed audit returns, randomized invitations and *external* information?

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **R93 · Same observed law, different hidden populations**

  Even near-complete response does not force point identification when completion depends on the unreturned binary outcome.

  [Exact observational equivalence](../articles/w3-r93-exact-observed-law-nonidentification.md)

- **R94 · Guaranteed completion floors**

  An *externally defensible* lower bound on each outcome's completion probability can conditionally narrow sharp latent-mean intervals. Incompatible floors must be rejected.

  [Exact floor sensitivity](../articles/w3-r94-external-audit-completion-bounds.md)

- **R95 · Know the direction—or admit you do not**

  A known direction of selective completion can halve a symmetric identified set; monotonic selection without a known sign supplies no reduction.

  [Exact directional sets](../articles/w3-r95-monotone-audit-return-identification.md)

</div>

![R95 conditional directional identification figure](../assets/w3-r95-monotone-direction-sharp-sets.svg)

**Interpretation:** these are *conditional identified sets*, **not confidence intervals**. R95 comprises 54 deterministic rational cases, **not** 54 independent datasets. Its original [PR #462](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/462) and independent [PR #463](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/pull/463) remain draft/unmerged. ## What did not change

- **Published stable package:** 1.1.0, Python 3.11–3.13.
- **Unpublished development source:** 1.2.0rc2.dev0, not a separately published qualified literal RC2.
- **Research blockers:** [#289](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/289) (simultaneous bands) and [#296](https://github.com/stefanosbalaskas/eyetrajectoriespy/issues/296) (observation-dependent gaze).
- **GitHub Release / PyPI for 1.2:** on hold.

The [site and research evidence](../research/index.md) are intended to expose unqualified or negative results clearly—not to turn them into advertised stable functionality.
